# /launch-checklist

No account needed. This is the timeline Hustlin stages when it plans a launch; with the `hustlin` server connected, `/launch` creates it with drafted assets and tracked links.

## Product Hunt (the anchor launch)

**Two weeks before**
- Create a maker account on producthunt.com with a real photo and bio. Only the maker can submit.
- Prepare 3 to 5 gallery images or GIFs of the product in action (1270x760) and a 240x240 logo.
- List 20 to 50 people who will comment on launch day. Ask each one personally. Thoughtful comments beat upvotes.

**One week before**
- Write the tagline, the description and the first comment (why you built it, who it is for, what is different).
- Schedule the launch for a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday at 12:01 am PT.
- Draft the rally messages for your supporters, one per person, not a blast.

**Launch day**
- Post the first comment the minute it goes live.
- Send rally messages staggered through the day.
- Reply to every comment within 30 minutes.
- Post on X, LinkedIn and the communities where your users are, with the launch link.

**After**
- Thank every supporter personally and share the result.
- Follow up with the signups that come over the next days.
- Write the retrospective. The retrospective is itself marketing.

## Show HN

- Build a little karma first by commenting usefully; brand-new accounts get flagged.
- Title "Show HN: <name> – <what it does in plain words>". No marketing adjectives.
- Post the explanatory top-level comment immediately: what, why, how it was built, what you want feedback on.
- Founder replies to every comment. Automation gets flagged here; this one is yours.
- Post around 8 to 10 am ET on a weekday.

## BetaList

- Submit early; listings take days to go live.
- The landing page needs a clear value proposition and an email capture before the listing lands.
- Share the listing when it is live and email every signup within 24 hours.

## Indie Hackers

- Engage in the community for a week before posting.
- Post the launch as a story with numbers and the problem it solves.
- Reply to every comment; that thread is where feedback lives.

## Order

Directories first (see `/directories`), BetaList and Indie Hackers in the same week, Product Hunt as the anchor, Show HN the week after when the product can take the traffic.
